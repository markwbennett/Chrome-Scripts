- **Last activity**: 2026-08-18

## Identity

Chrome MV3 extension (load unpacked). Content script on `search.txcourts.gov`; service worker fetches and caches both status maps from iacls.org.

### CCA colors

| Stage key | Label | Color |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| `granted_pending` | Granted | yellow `#f5e663` |
| `submitted` | Submitted | green `#7dcea0` |
| `decided` | Decided | grey `#b0b0b0` |
| `refused` | Refused | red `#f1948a` |

CCA stage classification reuses the ladder in `export_timeline_data.py` (docket `PDR DISP` / submit / opinion events; does not treat extension grants as PDR grants).

### COA colors (Bennett cases)

| Stage key | Label | Color |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| `pending` | Pending | yellow `#f5e663` |
| `submitted` | Submitted | green `#7dcea0` |
| `decided` | Decided | grey `#b0b0b0` |
| `dismissed` | Dismissed | red `#f1948a` |

COA stages come from TAMES docket events: dismissed (appeal/petition dismissed), decided (opinion issued or original-proceeding grant/deny), submitted (`Submitted` / past set-for-submission), else pending. Attorney-withdrawal and extension grants are ignored.
